Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Cuisine
Italian

Ingredients ＋ add

  • 4boneless chicken breasts
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1 cupheavy cream
  • ½ cupsun-dried tomatoes
  • 2 cupsfresh spinach
  • ½ cupgrated parmesan
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced

Method ＋ add step

  1. Season the chicken generously. Sear in olive oil over medium-high heat, 4–5 minutes per side, until deeply golden. Set aside to rest.

  2. Lower the heat, add the garlic, and let it bloom. Pour in the cream and broth, then whisk in parmesan and Italian seasoning.

  3. Fold through the sun-dried tomatoes and spinach; simmer gently until the sauce coats a spoon and the greens collapse.

  4. Return the chicken to the pan, spoon the sauce over, and simmer two minutes more. Serve at once.
