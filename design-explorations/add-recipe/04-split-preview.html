Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

by you · Italian · Dinner

Juicy pan-seared chicken in a rich sun-dried tomato cream sauce with spinach and parmesan. On the table in 30 minutes.

Serves
4
Prep
10m
Cook
20m
Per serving
$3.40

Ingredients

  • 4boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 cupheavy cream
  • ½ cupsun-dried tomatoes
  • 2 cupsfresh spinach

Method

  1. Season and sear the chicken until golden, then set aside.
  2. Bloom the garlic, then build the cream sauce with broth and parmesan.
  3. Fold in tomatoes and spinach, return the chicken, and simmer to finish.
