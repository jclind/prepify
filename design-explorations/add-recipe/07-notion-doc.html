Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

🍽 Servings4
⏱ Prep · Cook10 min · 20 min
🌍 CuisineItalian
📂 CourseDinnerMain course

Description

Juicy pan-seared chicken in a rich sun-dried tomato cream sauce with spinach and aged parmesan. Weeknight comfort, on the table in thirty minutes.

Ingredients

⋮⋮4 boneless chicken breasts
⋮⋮2 tbsp olive oil
⋮⋮1 cup heavy cream
⋮⋮½ cup sun-dried tomatoes
⋮⋮2 cups fresh spinach
⋮⋮½ cup grated parmesan
+ Add ingredient

Method

⋮⋮1.Season the chicken, then sear in olive oil over medium-high heat until golden, 4–5 minutes per side. Set aside.
⋮⋮2.Add the garlic to the pan and cook until fragrant, then pour in cream and broth and whisk in the parmesan.
⋮⋮3.Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes and spinach; simmer until the sauce thickens and the spinach wilts.
⋮⋮4.Return the chicken, spoon the sauce over the top, and simmer two minutes more before serving.
